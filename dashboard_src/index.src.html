Method, policy levers and limits

How to read this dashboard

Step 1 grows each region's FY2025-26 generation with income per person × electricity elasticity (~1.0, fading 0–20%), population growth and a small EV/data-centre uplift, across 20,000 Monte Carlo draws. Step 2 allocates the national path by source to regions with a seven-factor score (cost, resource, environment, security, infrastructure, policy, land) and RAS balancing, across 150 draws; coal is the balancing source. Bands show P10–P90 of the draws.

Policy levers and their sources
Limits to keep in mind

Wind and solar are limited by an integration ceiling (a storage/grid proxy), which sets the national split; coal therefore still rises in absolute terms before falling in some variants. Capacity-plan targets come from secondary sources and are converted to energy with load factors (solar 22%, wind 30%). Northeast gas is an artefact of small numbers. Sensitivity cases exist for India only. Map shows the five CEA regions; Andaman & Nicobar (in East) and Lakshadweep (in South) are drawn as inset islands; Lakshadweep is shown as schematic dots at approximate island positions.
